Discount Tire was founded in 1960 by Bruce Halle in Ann Arbor, Michigan. He started with just six tires and a tiny loan of around $400. Bruce Halle was the only employee at first — handling sales, tire installation, accounting, and janitorial duties himself.

The actual corporate name is Reinalt-Thomas Corporation, though the company is universally known as Discount Tire. In California and some other states, the company operates under the name America's Tire because another business already owned the Discount Tire name there.

Discount Tire is still privately owned, controlled by the Halle family, and has never gone public. It is the largest independent tire retailer in the United States.

As of 2025, the company operates over 1,200 stores across nearly 40 states and employs more than 30,000 people. Bruce Halle became the richest person in Arizona through the company, with Forbes estimating his net worth around $6 billion.

Discount Tire's most famous commercial features an elderly woman throwing a tire through a store window. It first aired in 1975 and became one of the longest-running TV commercials in history, earning a Guinness World Record.

The company has a strong internal promotion culture. Leadership strongly believes that store managers and executives should start their careers in the service bays changing tires. Promotion from within is a foundational value.

In 2021, Discount Tire acquired Tire Rack, one of the largest online tire retailers and independent tire testing organizations in the country. This was a landmark move that significantly expanded their online and research capabilities.

Discount Tire also owns Treadwell Research Park, a massive tire testing facility in Texas used to independently evaluate tire performance and quality.

While Discount Tire does not manufacture tires, it sells several exclusive tire brands and models available only through their stores.

Discount Tire has a long history in motorsports, sponsoring Team Penske in NASCAR for many years.

Bruce Halle's personal philosophy guided the company culture: "Be honest, work hard, have fun, be grateful, and pay it forward." This philosophy remains central to how the company operates today.
